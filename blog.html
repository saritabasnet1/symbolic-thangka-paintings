In short: five long articles — symbolic thangka meaning, the Wheel of Life, the Life of Buddha, Jataka tales, and choosing and caring for a thangka at home.

Five long articles, each written to be read slowly. Start with the first article for an introduction to symbolic thangka painting, then follow the subjects in order — or choose the subject that interests you most and begin there. Each article links to the others, so you can move between them as you read.

  • 1. Symbolic Thangka Paintings: Meaning, History and Buddhist Teaching

    What a symbolic thangka is, how teaching paintings differ from single-deity portraits, and how they explain karma, rebirth and the path out of suffering.

  • 2. Wheel of Life in Symbolic Thangka: Reading the Bhavachakra Step by Step

    A step-by-step reading of the Bhavachakra — its hub, its rings, its six realms and the figure who holds the wheel.

  • 3. Life of Buddha Thangka: How a Painting Tells the Buddha's Story

    How artists arrange the main episodes of the Buddha's life across a single painting, in order, scene by scene.

  • 4. Jataka Tales in Thangka Art: Stories, Symbols and Moral Lessons

    The Jataka tales as teaching stories: how small scenes carry moral lessons about generosity, patience and wisdom.

  • 5. How to Choose, Care for and Display a Symbolic Thangka at Home

    How to look, choose, hang and care for a symbolic thangka, with practical guidance for a home shrine or study room.
